This repository holds the institutional static site — a Vantablack, monochrome, Swiss-refinery aesthetic built without a framework, billed as the digital front of a ₹50-crore-class trading desk.

## What the desk moves

- **Silver Bullion** — bars, grain and industrial silver via verified refiners
- **Precious Metals** — gold and bullion through refinery-aligned channels
- **Industrial Commodities** — copper, aluminium and extended sourcing
- **Trade Sourcing** — manufacturer discovery, quotation management, verification

## Trading Desk Timeline

1. **Receive RFQ** — the buyer submits quantity, purity and city.
2. **Verification** — the desk validates buyer and requirement.
3. **Competitive Quotations** — 3–5 verified suppliers submit offers.
4. **Settlement** — buyer selects supplier, supplier fulfils, brokerage becomes payable.

## For suppliers

Genuine RFQs, no upfront listing fees, nationwide buyer access, and confidential quotation handling. Every supplier is manually verified before joining the network.

```
img/               — brushed-metal + grain textures (SVG), monochrome desk photography
sounds/            — synthesized metal-click, paper-swipe, soft-tick
assets/fonts/      — self-hosted Cormorant Garamond + Inter (woff2)
```

## Design notes

- Absolute Vantablack `#000000`; `#090909` surfaces; `#2A2A2A` borders; polished silver `#C8CCD1`.
- No CSS gradients anywhere — all metallic texture is SVG brush-stroke grain at 1.5–2% opacity.
- Polished silver, engraved logo treatment; glass navigation at 70% opacity with a 1 px silver border.
- Sections capped to ~820 px; 96 px vertical rhythm; two-column layouts throughout; no dead vertical space.
- Self-hosted fonts, lazy-loaded imagery, deferred audio — quiet and fast.
